References – Graph Neural Networks
Introduction and Recent Survey
1
Introduction to Graph Neural Networks
Sanchez-Lengeling, B., Reif, E., Pearce, A., & Wiltschko, A. B. (2021). “A Gentle Introduction to Graph Neural Networks.” Distill, 6(8).
Contribution: visual and interactive introduction to GNNs: graphs as data, the building blocks of a GNN, message passing and the node-, edge- and graph-level prediction tasks.
Online article: https://distill.pub/2021/gnn-intro/ · DOI: https://doi.org/10.23915/distill.00033
2
Recent Progress and Open Challenges
Zhang, X., Li, S., Li, H., Gong, P., Shi, J., Shen, J., Tian, C., Zhang, D., & Zhu, Q. (2026). “A Survey on Graph Neural Networks.” IEEE Access, 14, 27491–27512.
Contribution: systematic review of GNN progress since 2022 (architectures, optimization strategies, applications) and of open challenges such as scalability and dynamic graphs.
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1109/ACCESS.2026.3665657
PDF: https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/iel8/6287639/11323511/11397632.pdf
