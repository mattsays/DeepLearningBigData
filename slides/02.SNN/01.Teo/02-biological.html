3. Action potential
Input charges U(t) while it leaks away; crossing θ fires an all-or-nothing ~100 mV spike.
\(\displaystyle \tau\,\frac{dU}{dt} = -U(t) + I_{in}(t)\,R\)
Key idea: information lies in whether and when a neuron spikes, not in the spike's shape.
