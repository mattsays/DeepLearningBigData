ANN-to-SNN conversion

Train an ANN with differentiable activations.

Reuse the learned connectivity and weights.

Replace compatible activations with spiking neurons.

Calibrate activation scales and firing thresholds.

Focus on one corresponding neuron in each network.

Represent the ANN activation with spikes over time.

Inspect a short observation window.

Extend the window to refine the rate estimate.

Use standard backpropagation to learn the ANN weights.

Transfer the learned parameters to a compatible spiking architecture.

Corresponding neurons retain the learned connections.

Align the scales so spike rates approximate ANN activity.

A normalized activation can be represented by the firing rate of an IF neuron.

Count emitted spikes across the observation window.

First 3 steps: one spike gives r ≈ 0.33.

More observation steps can improve the match, at the cost of latency.
