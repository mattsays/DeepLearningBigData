06 - LIMITATIONS & CONCLUSION

Conclusion: spikes trade density for efficiency

1

SNNs compute with timed binary events. LIF neurons, rate/temporal codes, and event-driven accumulate-only inference replace dense MACs — silence costs nothing.

2

Training is solved well enough to scale. Surrogate-gradient BPTT, conversion, and hybrid pipelines now produce 80% ImageNet backbones and 7B–76B spiking LLMs.

3

The wins land first at the edge. On neuromorphic chips, SNNs deliver 30–1000× energy advantages in vision, audio, radar, and optimization — today, not in theory.

Where the field is heading

  • Spiking foundation models — SpikingBrain shows a non-Transformer path for long-context AI
  • On-chip continual learning — 70× faster, 5,600× more energy-efficient than GPU baselines on Loihi 2 (2025)
  • Hybrid ANN–SNN systems — each paradigm used where it is strongest
  • Hardware–software co-design and standard benchmarks (NeuroBench)
Bottom line: SNNs are no longer a neuroscience curiosity — they are a working, measurable route to AI that computes only when there is something worth computing.
Sources: Hajizadeh et al., Loihi 2 continual learning (2025); neuromorphic market analysis (2025); SpikingBrain (2025)
