GNN: The unfair advantage

Graphs can model almost any kind of data.

Traditional Data

Images form grid graphs: pixels are nodes and neighboring pixels are connected. Text forms a chain, with a node for each word. Even traditional data can be represented as graphs.

Heterogeneous Data

Atoms and bonds form molecular graphs; people and relationships form social graphs. GNNs learn from these connections, combining node features with information from neighbors to make predictions.
