Permutation Equivariance and Invariance
A graph has no canonical node ordering
Unlike sequences or grids, graph nodes have no inherent ordering: indices are arbitrary labels, not meaningful positions.
Equivariance · node outputs
\(\displaystyle f(PX,PAP^{\top})=P\,f(X,A)\)
Outputs are reordered, but each stays with its node.
Invariance · graph output
\(\displaystyle g(PX,PAP^{\top})=g(X,A)\)
The prediction is unchanged by any reordering.
Node-level tasks → permutation equivariance
Graph-level tasks → permutation invariance
