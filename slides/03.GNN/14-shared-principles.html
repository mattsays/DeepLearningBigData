Shared Principles
Common Foundations of SNNs and GNNs
1 · Local Interactions
Computation happens between connected units, not between every pair of units.
2 · Distributed Processing
Information is spread across many interconnected units.
3 · Learnable Parameters
Trainable weights transform incoming information; learning tunes them.
4 · Information Integration
Both combine multiple sources to update an internal representation.
Both architectures rely on interconnected computational units, local information exchange, and learnable mechanisms for integrating information.
