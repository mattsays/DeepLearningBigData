GraphSAGE and Graph Attention Networks
Two alternative strategies for neighborhood aggregation
GraphSAGE
Graph Sample and Aggregate
\(\displaystyle h_v^{(k)}=\sigma\big(W^{(k)}\cdot[\,h_v^{(k-1)}\,\|\,\mathrm{AGG}(\{h_u^{(k-1)}\})\,]\big)\)
Samples a fixed-size neighbor subset: scalable. Learns aggregators instead of per-node embeddings.
Inductive: embeds unseen nodes from their features and neighborhoods.
Graph Attention Network (GAT)
\(\displaystyle e_{ij}=\mathrm{LeakyReLU}(a^{\top}[Wh_i\,\|\,Wh_j])\;\;\;\alpha_{ij}=\frac{e^{e_{ij}}}{\sum_k e^{e_{ik}}}\;\;\;h_i'=\sigma\Big(\sum_j\alpha_{ij}Wh_j\Big)\)
\(\alpha_{ij}\): learned weight from neighbor \(j\) to node \(i\). Multi-head attention combines several mechanisms.
Example: e = (1.0, 0.0) gives α ≈ (0.73, 0.27); the higher score contributes more.
